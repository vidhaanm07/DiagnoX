Why this version is more accurate

The prototype avoids pretending it understands questions it does not recognize.

◈ Context matching

Recognizes combinations such as “TV turns on but keeps restarting” instead of treating every TV question as “no picture.”

⌬ Clarification first

When the device or symptom is ambiguous, DiagnoX asks for the missing information instead of inventing a diagnosis.

◎ Closed-loop support

After guidance, the user confirms whether the fix worked; otherwise the case moves to escalation.
